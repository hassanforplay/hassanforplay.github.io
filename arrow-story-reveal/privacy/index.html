English

Effective 1 November 2026

Arrow Story Reveal is made by Hassan Nasar (“the developer”). This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

1. No account, no personal details. The game never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location, and it has no account or server of its own.

2. Your progress stays on your phone. Levels, points, achievements and settings are saved only on this device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them for good; the developer cannot see or restore them.

3. Ads. The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and how ads are used. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change it any time under Settings › Privacy and ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. Google’s use of this information is covered by Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Google Play Games (optional). If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends Google three numbers: your total best score, the levels you have cleared and your longest fire. They appear on leaderboards with your Play Games name and picture, where other players can see them. With your permission, the game also shows how you rank among your Play Games friends; it reads only which friends play and their scores, and keeps none of it. Google handles this under its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy), and you can choose who sees your profile, or sign out, in Play Games. Without signing in, nothing is sent.

5. Sharing. When you share a win, the game draws a picture on your phone and hands it to the app you pick; the game itself sends nothing. The link in a shared win carries only the level, your time and your score, so a friend can try to beat them. If you install the game from such a link, Google Play hands that challenge to the game the first time it opens, and the game uses it only to open the challenge.

6. Phone permissions. The game uses the internet only for ads and, if you sign in, Play Games, and vibration only for touch feedback. It reads none of your files and uses no camera or microphone.

7. Children. The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Security. The developer receives and stores no information about you. Information handled by Google, for ads and Play Games, is protected under Google’s policies.

9. Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the game with a new date.

10. Contact. Questions about privacy: hassanforplay@gmail.com
